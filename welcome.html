Welcome to UniShield

Your universal shield against feed pollution on Reddit, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn. Enjoy a clean, authentic, and completely silent browsing experience without distraction.

Runs silently in the background • 100% local & tracker-free

Reddit

Silently identifies bot patterns, suppresses political astroturfing, blocks spam, and filters posts and comments by custom muted keywords.

LinkedIn Feed & Jobs

Removes promoted ads, suggested posts, interaction noise ("liked this", "commented"), broetry, promoted job listings, detects fake profiles, and lets you save private notes on contacts.

Neutral Anti-Hate Filters

Balanced, objective protection against toxic "gender war" hostility (covering both misogyny and misandry) and universal anti-racism filtering covering all ethnicities without double standards.

Granular Customization

Tailor your experience with custom muted keywords, company blocklists, one-click popup controls, and full JSON configuration backup & restore.
