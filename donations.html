🤝 Why Support?

UniShield is a free, privacy-first, open-source extension that restores clean, authentic feeds on Reddit, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn. Your support helps me:

  • Continuously adapt DOM selectors as Reddit, X, and LinkedIn update their web interfaces
  • Expand and maintain neutral, comprehensive regex filters for hate speech, scams, and bot patterns
  • Keep the extension 100% free and open-source with zero trackers or telemetry
  • Maintain high-speed silent performance so your browsing remains smooth and uninterrupted
